# About

## What this site is

Economie publishes long-form analyses of economies — so far Romania,
Greece, the Romanian IT sector and China's industrial development —
built from public statistics. The reports are descriptive and
data-first: they set out what the numbers show, the main competing
interpretations, and where the evidence runs out.

## Method

- **Public sources only.** Data comes from official and widely used
  public sources: Eurostat, the World Bank, the ECB, the IMF, the BIS,
  the European Commission, the IEA, IRENA, Ember, the IAEA, USGS, WIPO,
  national statistics offices and company filings. A few figures come
  from reputable trade press where the primary source is paywalled;
  those are cited as such.
- **Reproducible charts.** Every chart is produced by a Python script
  that fetches or reads the underlying data. Each CSV records its
  source, URL and retrieval date.
- **Checked text.** Figures quoted in the text are checked against the
  same CSV files the charts use.
- **Updates and corrections.** Reports are updated as new data is
  published. Each update says what changed — including corrections to
  earlier editions — in a note at the top of the report.

The reports were researched and written with the help of AI tools
(Claude Code), under the rule that no figure goes into a report unless
it can be traced to a named public dataset and checked against it.
