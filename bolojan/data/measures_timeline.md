# Bolojan government — measures timeline

*Source-anchored chronology. Where a primary Monitorul Oficial / Consiliul Fiscal reference is missing, the entry is marked **[needs MO reference]** and the secondary citation is given.*

*Coverage window: 2025-06-23 (government sworn in) → 2026-05-05 (no-confidence vote).*

---

## Government formation

- **2025-05** — Nicușor Dan wins the presidential rerun and nominates Ilie Bolojan as Prime Minister.
- **2025-06-23** — Bolojan government sworn in. Four-party pro-EU coalition: PNL, PSD, USR, UDMR.

## Pachetul 1 — Legea 141/2025 (M.O. nr. 699 / 25 iulie 2025; effective from 2025-08-01)

**Legal trace:**
- 2025-07-07 — Government engages its responsibility (*asumarea răspunderii*) in joint plenary.
- 2025-07-25 — Published as *Legea nr. 141/2025 privind unele măsuri fiscal-bugetare* in **Monitorul Oficial, Partea I, nr. 699**.
- 2025-07-26 — Promulgated by President Nicușor Dan.
- 2025-08-01 — Main provisions take effect.

Revenue measures:

| Measure | Detail | Estimated impact |
|---|---|---|
| Standard VAT | 19% → **21%** | ~ main revenue lever |
| Reduced VAT | 5% and 9% rates unified at **11%** (food, medicines, water, books, firewood, district heating, hospitality) | — |
| Bank turnover tax | 2% → **4%** | — |
| Excise duties | +10% on fuels, alcohol, tobacco | — |
| Gambling levies | Higher tariffs | ~RON 500M in 2025 |
| Healthcare contributions | Extended to pensions above RON 3,000/month | Applies 2025-08-01 to 2027-12-31 |
| Dividend tax | 10% → 16% | Applies from 2026-01-01 |

Spending measures:

- Temporary cap on public-sector wages and pensions (following 2024 increases of ~20% for salaries and >30% for pensions).

## Pachetul 2 — *asumarea răspunderii* on 1 September 2025 (five laws of an originally six-part design)

**Legal trace:** to insulate the reforms from CCR challenge, the package was structured into six sub-packages, of which **five** were assumed jointly by the Government on **1 September 2025** in an extraordinary plenary session of Parliament. The sixth (local-administration reform) was withdrawn from the agenda before the session.

| # | Sub-package | Outcome | Lege | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fiscal measures and insolvency (core fiscal text) | Adopted | **Legea nr. 239/2025** | autumn 2025 |
| 2 | Corporate governance of state-owned enterprises (cap on board pay/bonuses; non-executive members earn ≤ 2× sectoral average wage) | Adopted, promulgated Oct 2025; CCR validated | *Lege no. to be filled from M.O.* | Oct 2025 |
| 3 | Reform of regulatory authorities (ANRE, ASF, ANCOM): 10% cut in specialist posts, 30% in support; no bonuses for board members through 31 Dec 2028 | Adopted, promulgated | *Lege no. to be filled from M.O.* | autumn 2025 |
| 4 | Healthcare reform: hospital-management reform, restructuring of medical units, full coverage for HIV/AIDS, TB, cancer day-hospital services | CCR validated; promulgated by President Nicușor Dan on **23 October 2025** | *Lege no. to be filled from M.O.* | 2025-10-23 |
| 5 | Magistrates' special pensions: cap at 70% of last net income; retirement age to 65 over 10-year transition (to ~2036) | **CCR struck down as unconstitutional**; Romania forfeited EUR 231 m of PNRR funds in Feb 2026 because of the missed milestone | — | — |
| 6 | Local-administration reform | **Withdrawn** from the *asumarea* agenda; not adopted under Pachetul 2 | — | — |

Aggregate Government-estimated budgetary impact of Pachetul 2 as approved: **~RON 3.7 bn**.

Sources: gov.ro press releases (2025-08-29; 2025-09-01); Europa Liberă; JURIDICE.ro; AGERPRES; Lege5.ro.

Public-sector restructuring:

- Civil-service headcount target: up to **20% reduction**, ~167,000 jobs.
- **Hazard pay** for ~1 million public-sector workers cut fivefold (estimated saving ~EUR 270M/year).
- Reform of **special pensions** (elimination of exceptions).
- Reform of **state-owned enterprises** and autonomous agencies.
- Downsizing of local and central administration via decentralisation and digitisation.

Revenue measures:

- Higher taxes on **profits** and on **energy** sectors.
- Re-evaluation of public investment projects to prioritise export growth.

Sources: Legea nr. 239/2025 (full text via Lege5 and JURIDICE.ro); PwC Romania Tax Alert *Pachet fiscal 2 — Legea nr. 239/2025*; Balkan Insight, *Romania Targets Civil Service and Business in New Austerity Measures*, 2025-08-29.

## Consiliul Fiscal opinion (Aug 2025 letter to Senate)

The independent Fiscal Council assessed both packages and concluded:

- Aggregate package impact: **RON 23.5 bn (1.23% of GDP) in 2025** and **RON 44.5 bn (2.18% of GDP) in 2026**.
- Without intervention, 2025 deficit risked returning to ~9% of GDP; the 7.04% target in the previous budget law was "far from reality".
- "There is no alternative to severe correction" — financing and refinancing access was at imminent risk.
- Even with the packages, 2026 deficit was assessed as potentially around 6.5% of GDP.

Source: Consiliul Fiscal, transmission letter to Senate Secretary General, August 2025 (`senat.ro/legis/PDF/2025/25b595CF.PDF`).

## Other notable developments under the Bolojan government

- **2025-08-15** — Fitch confirms Romania at **BBB- with negative outlook**, citing persistent fiscal pressures, high deficit, weak growth. (S&P and Moody's also stand at the lowest investment-grade notch with negative outlook.)
- **2025-10-22** — European Commission **approves Romania's revised PNRR** (total EUR 21.4B: EUR 13.57B grants + EUR 7.84B loans).
- **2025-11-12** — IMF concludes 2025 **Article IV consultation** with Romania.
- **2025-11-13** — Romania prepares **fourth PNRR payment request** of EUR 2.6B (grants only; 62 milestones, 26 reforms, 23 investments).
- **2026-02** — News that Romania will lose **EUR 231M of PNRR funds** because the reform of magistrates' pensions was not implemented on time (Constitutional Court delays).
- **2026-03** — **Natural-gas price cap removed**, named by analysts as a key disinflation headwind.

## End of the government

- **2026-04 (late)** — PSD withdraws from the coalition.
- **2026-05-05** — Parliament passes a no-confidence motion (filed jointly by PSD and AUR) by **281 to 4** — the highest support for such a motion in post-communist Romania. Bolojan continues as **interim PM with limited powers**. President Nicușor Dan opens negotiations to rebuild the four-party coalition.
- Immediately after the vote: **EUR/RON** weakens to a 20-year low; Romania's external-debt yields rise.
