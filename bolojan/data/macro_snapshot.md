**Q1 2026 cash-basis budget execution** (Ministry of Finance):
- Deficit: **RON 22.0 bn = 1.03% of GDP**, down from RON 44 bn = 2.3% of GDP in Q1 2025.
- Revenues: **RON 158.8 bn (+12.3% YoY)**; VAT receipts **+17.7%** (RON 33.6 bn) — driven by the August 2025 VAT-rate hikes.
- Expenditures: **RON 179.9 bn (-2.8% YoY)** — wage-and-pension freeze biting.

Real wages have now declined YoY for three consecutive quarters. The state forecasting body (CNP) projects real wage contractions of **-1.1% in 2025** and **-0.9% in 2026**. Public-sector wages and pensions remain frozen at November 2024 levels under the Pachetul 1 / Pachetul 2 framework, extended into 2026.

## PNRR / NRRP

- Revised plan approved by EC on **2025-10-22**: **EUR 21.4 bn** (EUR 13.57 bn grants + EUR 7.84 bn loans).
- Disbursed to date: **EUR 10.72 bn** (about half of total allocation).
- 4th payment request submitted ~2025-11-13: **EUR 2.6 bn** (grants only; 62 milestones, 26 reforms, 23 investments).
- 3 payment requests remain before the **2026-08** deadline.
- **EUR 231 m** of funding lost in early 2026 due to delays in the magistrates' pensions reform (Constitutional Court).
- **EUR 6.3 bn** of projects at risk of missing the 2026-08 deadline.
